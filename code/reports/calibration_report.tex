\noindent\small
The model is solved in per-capita detrended units: aggregates are divided by
$Z_tN_t$ with $Z_t=(1+g)^t$, so every stock recursion carries
$\Gamma=(1+g)(1+n)$ and the household budget carries $(1+g)$ on next-period
assets. Preferences are $\log c-\nu\ell^{1+\varphi}/(1+\varphi)$. Government
debt is external and official-sector: households hold domestic capital and
foreign assets at $r$; the government pays $r_B$ abroad.
A hat denotes a detrended per-capita quantity, $\hat x_t=X_t/(Z_tN_t)$; these
are constant along the balanced growth path, while the corresponding levels
$X_t$ grow at $\Gamma-1$ and per-capita terms at $g$. Total factor
productivity is set so that $\hat y=1$, which makes every ratio to output
readable as a level. Parameters below are annual.
